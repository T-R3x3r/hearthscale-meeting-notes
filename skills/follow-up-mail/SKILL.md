---
name: follow-up-mail
description: Use when the person asks for a follow-up mail after a meeting.
---

Read the meeting's notes with `read_meeting`. Write a short mail to the people of the meeting: thank them in one sentence, list the decisions and the next steps with who does each and when, and end with one line. Use only what the notes and the transcript say. Give the subject on the first line as "Subject: ...". The person sends it themselves.
